Google Play Data Safety Mandate

Account & Data Deletion Request

Application: Vandi (Kerala Bus Route Mapping)

1. Overview

Under Google Play's Data Safety policies, users who have registered an account on Vandi (via Google OAuth) have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of their account profile and associated personal data.

2. What Data Is Deleted vs Retained

Data Type Action Taken Details
Google Account ID & Email Permanently Deleted Removed from Supabase Auth identity table and session tokens invalidated immediately.
Display Name & Profile Avatar Permanently Deleted Erased from contributor profiles and trust score leaderboards.
Personal Contributor History Permanently Deleted Submission linkage to your identity is disconnected.
Public Transit Stops & Routes Anonymized Approved public transit schedules and stop coordinate markers remain in the public directory to maintain public timetable utility, but any attribution to your name or account is removed.

3. How to Request Deletion

You can request account deletion at any time without needing the app installed. Simply fill out the form below or send an email directly to privacy@vandi.community.

4. Turnaround Time

Account deletion requests are processed by our automated database routines and administrative moderation team within 7 business days. Once finalized, a confirmation will be dispatched to your registered email address.
