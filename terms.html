2. Nature of Service & Transit Disclaimer

Vandi is a community-driven transit directory and route mapping utility providing information about public (KSRTC) and private stage carriage bus schedules across Kerala.

Notice Regarding Timings and Schedules: Bus departure times, routes, stops, and operator schedules are crowdsourced from commuter contributions and publicly displayed route boards. Actual bus operations may vary due to road traffic conditions, weather events, regulatory revisions, or depot operational changes. Vandi is not affiliated with the Kerala State Road Transport Corporation (KSRTC) or any private bus transport union. The app is intended for informational convenience only.

3. Contributor Guidelines & Community Submissions

Users who register with Google OAuth to submit new routes, update timings, or upload destination board proofs agree to:

  • Submit accurate, truthful, and verified route information to the best of their knowledge.
  • Upload only authentic photos of buses and route destination boards taken by themselves or from legitimate public signage.
  • Refrain from uploading obscene, defamatory, illegal, or copyrighted non-transit imagery.
  • Refrain from spamming duplicate routes, intentionally vandalizing stop sequences, or manipulating trust votes.

Vandi maintainers and administrators reserve the absolute right to review, edit, reject, or remove any submission, and to suspend contributor accounts that violate these guidelines.
