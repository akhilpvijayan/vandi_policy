Help & Support

Application: Vandi (Kerala Bus Route Mapping)

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I find bus timings and stops?
You can use the home search bar to enter origin and destination stops (e.g. Aluva to Perumbavoor) or vehicle registration numbers. The app displays sequenced stops, estimated timings, and operator class (KSRTC Ordinary/Fast or Private Bus).
2. Can I use Vandi without creating an account?
Yes! All search, timetable discovery, stop inspection, and map viewing features work completely in Guest Mode without signing in. You only need to sign in with Google if you want to propose new routes or submit updates.
3. Why does submitting a route require photo proof?
To maintain high trust and eliminate inaccurate timings, our community moderation guidelines require a photo of the bus route board or vehicle exterior. Submissions are reviewed before becoming publicly listed.
4. How can I report an incorrect bus timing or missing stop?
On any bus detail screen, tap the "Dispute / Inaccurate" button to notify the community and trigger review. You can also propose updated stop sequences directly through the "Edit Route" option.
5. How do I delete my profile?
Visit our Account Deletion Page to request deletion of your account and personal contributor history.
