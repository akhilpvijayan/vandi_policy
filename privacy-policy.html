Privacy Policy

Application: Vandi (Kerala Bus Route Mapping) • Effective Date: October 2026

1. Introduction

Vandi ("we", "our", or "the App") is an open-source, community-driven transit mapping and route discovery platform designed for public commuters in Kerala, covering both private stage-carriage buses and KSRTC (Kerala State Road Transport Corporation) services.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information collected through your use of the application. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, usage, third-party disclosure, and user data rights pursuant to Google Play Developer Policy requirements.

2. Data We Collect & Access

Depending on how you use Vandi, we may collect and process the following categories of information:

Data Category How It Is Collected Purpose of Use
Account Profile (Google OAuth) Provided voluntarily when you sign in via Google OAuth Displays your public contributor name and attribution badge when proposing bus routes or stops.
Photos & Camera Proofs Captured or chosen from your device gallery by you Verification proof of bus front route boards and vehicle registration numbers before approving community submissions.
Geographic Coordinates (Stops) Selected on the map or dropped as a pin by the user To map bus stop coordinates (latitude & longitude) using PostGIS to render route polylines.
Crash & Diagnostic Data Automated system error logs Troubleshooting app crashes and performance stabilization.

No Real-Time Background Location Tracking: Vandi does NOT track or monitor your device's background location or physical movements. Stop coordinates are only saved when you explicitly select or drop a pin for a bus stop.

3. Device Permissions Requested

The app requests the following Android runtime permissions only when relevant features are accessed:

  • CAMERA: Requested solely when you take a live photo of a bus exterior or destination board for submission proof.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: Requested to select pre-existing route board photos from your gallery for route verification.
  • INTERNET: Required to query bus schedules, search bus stops, and synchronize crowdsourced transit routes.

4. Client-Side Image Compression & Security

To protect your mobile bandwidth and storage, photos uploaded as route proof are compressed client-side directly on your device prior to transmission. Images are stored securely on Supabase Storage with cryptographic tokens.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

Vandi leverages reputable, privacy-focused open infrastructure to run the service:

  • Google OAuth (Supabase Auth): Facilitates frictionless sign-in. Google's privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Free-tier open-source PostgreSQL database and object storage. Data is hosted securely with Row Level Security (RLS) enabled.
  • OpenFreeMap & MapLibre GL: We use OpenFreeMap for vector map tiles. OpenFreeMap operates with zero tracking, zero cookies, and zero user profiling.

6. Guest Mode (No Account Required)

You can use Vandi completely as a guest without creating an account or signing in. Guest users have full access to search bus routes, view timing timetables, browse bus stops, and inspect route maps without disclosing any personal information. Sign-in is only required if you choose to submit new routes or contribute updates.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety and Account Deletion policy:

  • You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.
  • Upon account deletion, your contributor profile, personal identity, and authentication records are permanently erased from our systems.
  • Crowdsourced public bus schedule data (stop sequences and public timings) may remain anonymized to preserve public transit directory utility.

To submit a deletion request online, visit our Account Deletion Request Page or email us at privacy@vandi.community.

8. Children's Privacy

Vandi is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected, we will promptly delete it.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
